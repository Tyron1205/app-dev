#  The Greatest Showman
### Cast:
- *Hugh Jackman as P.T. Barnum*
- *Zac Efron as Phillip Carlyle*
- *Michelle Williams as Charity Barnum*
- *Rebecca Ferguson as Jenny Lind (singing voice by Loren Allred)*
- *Zendaya as Anne Wheeler*
- *Keala Settle as Lettie Lutz (The Bearded Lady)*

### Summary:
*The Greatest Showman is a musical inspired by the life of P.T. Barnum, the visionary who founded the Barnum & Bailey Circus. The film follows Barnum’s journey from humble beginnings to becoming a show business pioneer.
He creates a circus featuring performers who are often outcasts from society — like the bearded lady, the tallest man, and acrobats — and turns their uniqueness into a spectacle that celebrates diversity and imagination.*
